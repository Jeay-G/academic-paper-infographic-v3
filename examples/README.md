## Glioma and CD14+ monocytes

The three examples below present the same medical research topic with different visual treatments: a structured editorial long image, a scientific illustration-led image, and a compact evidence summary. The figures retain the source notes and limitations shown in the original artwork; they are examples of visual communication, not medical advice.
